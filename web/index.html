Headless TV-B-Gone

Flash the latest firmware to an ESP32 directly from your browser in Chrome or Edge.

Warning: flashing replaces the board's existing firmware. Only flash devices you own or are authorized to control.

North America / Asia

Use the NA/Asia firmware.

Europe

Use the EU firmware.

Requires the ESP32 board, USB data cable, and IR LED connected to GPIO 4. Use a correct resistor or transistor driver for the LED.
