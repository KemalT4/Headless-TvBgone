Headless TV-B-Gone

ESP32 infrared transmitter web flasher

Use only on equipment you own or are authorized to control. Flashing replaces the board's existing firmware.

North America / Asia

Supports ESP32, ESP32-S2, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3, and ESP32-C6. The installer selects the matching image for your connected chip.

Europe

Supports ESP32, ESP32-S2, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3, and ESP32-C6. The installer selects the matching image for your connected chip.

Use Chrome or Edge over HTTPS. Connect with a USB data cable. The IR LED is configured for GPIO 4.
